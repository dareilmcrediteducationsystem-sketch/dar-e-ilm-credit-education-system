Academics

Academics, Playgroup to Class 5

تعلیمی مراحل

A step-by-step journey where each stage builds the next, strong basics, spoken confidence and beautiful character at every level.

Playgroup

Ages 3+

A joyful first step into school, learning through play, rhymes, colours and kindness.

  • Play-based learning & motor skills
  • Rhymes, stories & daily duas
  • Sharing, manners & confidence

Nursery & Prep (KG)

Ages 4–5

Letters, sounds and numbers come alive, the reading and writing foundation begins.

  • Phonics: English & Urdu sounds
  • Number concepts & neat writing
  • Nazra Quran & moral stories

Class 1 & 2

Ages 6–7

Fluent reading, clear writing and confident maths, with curiosity encouraged every day.

  • English, Urdu, Maths & General Knowledge
  • Reading fluency & handwriting
  • Islamiyat & good-habit routines

Class 3 to 5

Ages 8–10

Deeper concepts, spoken English, leadership roles, fully ready for middle school and beyond.

  • Science, Social Studies & Computer basics
  • Presentations, quizzes & sports
  • Discipline, leadership & responsibility

Online Portals

Teacher & Parent Portals

ٹیچر اور والدین پورٹل

One simple portal for our teachers and one for our parents, so attendance, homework and results are always close at hand.

Teacher Portal

ٹیچر پورٹل

Everything a Dar-e-Ilm teacher needs for the school day, all in one place.

  • 1-tap attendance for the whole class
  • Homework and assignments, set in seconds
  • Marks that go straight onto the report card
  • A daily class diary that parents can read
  • Class timetable, with the day's lectures at a glance
  • Direct messages with parents
Get your login from the school office

Call or WhatsApp 0346-7969310 and the office will create your teacher account.

Parent Portal

والدین پورٹل

Young children have no login of their own. The parent portal is the child's portal, and every parent sees only their own child.

  • Only your own child's attendance, day by day and for the whole month
  • Homework and assignments, with due dates
  • Report card with marks, total, percentage and grade
  • The teacher's daily diary notes
  • Class timings and school notices
  • Message the class teacher directly, and get a reply
Get your login from the school office

Call or WhatsApp 0346-7969310 and the office will create your account, linked to your child.

Parent FAQ

Questions Parents Ask Us

Honest answers, and for anything specific to your child's class, one WhatsApp message gets you the exact detail.

What is the fee?

Fees depend on the class, from Playgroup to Class 5, so we don't publish a fixed figure here, you always get the current one from us directly. Ask us on WhatsApp or call 0346-7969310 and we will share the full fee structure.

Is transport available?

Please ask us on WhatsApp, tell us your area, and we will confirm what transport options are available for your child.

What about the uniform?

Students wear the Dar-e-Ilm navy and white uniform. For sizes and where to get it, ask us on WhatsApp or ask at the campus when you visit.

What are the school timings?

Our school timings are 8:00 to 1:30, and on Friday 8:00 to 12:00.

What should we bring for admission?

If your child attended a previous school, bring that school's record with you. For the complete document list for your child's class, ask us on WhatsApp, we will tell you exactly what to bring, so one visit is enough.
